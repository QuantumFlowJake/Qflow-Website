1. Information We Collect

We collect information in the following ways:

Information you provide to us

When you fill out a form on our website, book a call, communicate with us, or become a client, you may provide:

  • Your name and business/company name
  • Your email address
  • Your mobile phone number (if you choose to provide it)
  • The contents of any message or inquiry you send us
  • Billing and account details necessary to provide our services

Information collected automatically

When you visit our website, we (and our hosting/analytics providers) may automatically collect limited technical information such as your IP address, browser type, device information, pages viewed, and referring URLs. This is used to operate, secure, and improve the site.

Information we process on behalf of our clients

As part of our done-for-you outreach services, we build and operate outreach campaigns on behalf of our business clients. In that role we may process business-contact information (such as names, business email addresses, and company details of a client's prospects). We process this information as a service provider on our clients' instructions; the client is responsible for the lawful basis of their campaigns.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect to:

  • Respond to your inquiries and provide the services you request
  • Set up, deliver, manage, and support your account and campaigns
  • Send you account, service, onboarding, appointment, and campaign-related communications, including by email and — where you have opted in — by SMS
  • Process payments and manage billing
  • Operate, maintain, secure, and improve our website and services
  • Comply with legal obligations and enforce our agreements

3. SMS / Text Messaging

If you provide your mobile number and opt in, QuantumFlow AI may send you text (SMS) messages. Our messaging program includes two types of recipients: (a) our clients, who receive account, onboarding, billing, and campaign-related notifications; and (b) leads and prospects who have expressly opted in through a web form to receive follow-up messages.

  • Consent. You opt in by checking the SMS consent box on our contact form and providing your mobile number, or by giving express consent during onboarding. Consent is not a condition of purchasing any product or service.
  • Message frequency. Message frequency varies depending on your interactions with us and your campaign activity.
  • Cost. Message and data rates may apply, according to your mobile carrier's plan.
  • Opt-out. You can cancel at any time by replying STOP to any message. After you send STOP, we will send a single confirmation and will not send you further SMS messages unless you opt in again.
  • Help. Reply HELP for assistance, or email us at Info@quantumflowai.net.
  • Carriers. Mobile carriers are not liable for delayed or undelivered messages.

No sharing of mobile opt-in data. No mobile information — including your phone number, SMS opt-in, or consent — will be shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes. Text messaging originator opt-in data and consent are never sold, rented, or shared with any third party for their own purposes. This information is used solely to deliver the messages you have requested and to operate our messaging program, and is only disclosed to the subprocessors that transmit our messages on our behalf (for example, our SMS platform and telecommunications providers).

4. How We Share Information

We do not sell your personal information. We may share information only in these limited circumstances:

  • Service providers (subprocessors). With vendors that help us operate our business — for example, website hosting, form processing, email delivery, our CRM, payment processing, and our SMS/telecommunications providers — solely so they can perform services for us and under obligations of confidentiality. As noted above, mobile opt-in data and consent are only shared with the providers that transmit messages on our behalf, never for those providers' own marketing.
  • Legal and safety. When required by law, subpoena, or legal process, or to protect the rights, property, or safety of QuantumFlow AI, our clients, or others.
  • Business transfers. In connection with a merger, acquisition, financing, or sale of assets, subject to this policy.
  • With your direction. When you ask us to share information with a third party.

5. Data Retention

We retain personal information for as long as needed to provide our services, maintain records of consent, comply with our legal obligations, resolve disputes, and enforce our agreements. When information is no longer needed, we take reasonable steps to delete or de-identify it.

6. Your Choices & Rights

  • SMS. Reply STOP to any text message to stop receiving SMS at any time.
  • Email. You can unsubscribe from marketing emails using the link in the email or by contacting us.
  • Access, correction, and deletion. Depending on your location, you may have the right to request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal information, and to opt out of certain processing. To exercise these rights, email Info@quantumflowai.net. We will respond consistent with applicable law and will not discriminate against you for exercising your rights.

7. Data Security

We use reasonable administrative, technical, and organizational measures designed to protect personal information against loss, misuse, and unauthorized access. However, no method of transmission or storage is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

8. Cookies & Tracking

Our website may use cookies and similar technologies to remember your preferences, understand how the site is used, and improve performance. You can control cookies through your browser settings; disabling cookies may affect some features of the site.

9. Children's Privacy

Our website and services are intended for businesses and individuals aged 18 and older. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided us information, please contact us so we can delete it.

10. Changes to This Policy
